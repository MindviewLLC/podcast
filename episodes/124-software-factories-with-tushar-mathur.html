About this episode

We chat with Tushar Mathur, founder & CEO of ForgeCode, about software factories: systems of agents aimed at an organization's goals rather than one developer's productivity. We get into dark vs. lights-on factories, the primitives a factory needs, self-improvement loops, accountability, selfware, and moving factories off of Git. We also talk more than usual, because we're excited about this stuff.
